01 · Executive Summary

About the semester project

A digital advertising campaign designed to build awareness and encourage Iowa State students to explore Morning Bell’s seasonal coffee offer.

Throughout the semester, this project focuses on developing a connected digital campaign for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. The campaign is aimed at Iowa State students in Ames and promotes new fall flavors with a 15% discount offer. The work includes identifying the target audience, defining the campaign goal and hook, developing display-ad creative, and creating a landing page that continues the message from the ads.

The website brings these pieces together in one place. Visitors can review the campaign details and click through to the landing page from the Ads page. Keeping the audience, offer, colors, and call to action consistent helps create a clearer experience from the first ad impression to the offer details.

02 · Semester Work

From campaign planning to landing page

The project connects the key steps needed to plan and present a digital display-ad campaign.

01

Define the audience

Focus the campaign on Iowa State students in Ames who may be interested in coffee and seasonal flavors.

02

Set the message

Use a clear hook, promotional offer, campaign goal, and call to action to make the ads easy to understand.

03

Develop the ads

Present four ad versions with different visual treatments while keeping the offer and main message consistent.

04

Connect the landing page

Route visitors from each ad to one dedicated landing page with matching visuals and offer information.
