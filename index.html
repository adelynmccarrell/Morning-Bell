Executive Summary

What We Accomplished This Semester

Campaign Foundation

We developed a complete display advertising campaign for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters with a focus on local awareness, product relevance, and a welcoming brand identity grounded in coffee culture and community.

Audience Strategy

Our targeting strategy centered on adults aged 22–54 in and around Ames, including students, young professionals, faculty, and commuters who value convenient, high-quality coffee experiences.

Creative Development

We created four ad variations built around a consistent message: fresh, local coffee that helps people start their morning with energy and comfort.

Conversion Focus

Every ad directs users to a dedicated landing page that reinforces the offer, the brand story, and the call to action, connecting ad exposure to real interest.

Brand Positioning

Built for the rhythm of Ames.

Morning Bell is positioned as a comforting, approachable, and locally rooted coffee shop that fits into the routines of busy mornings, midday resets, and everyday community life. The campaign emphasizes warmth, quality, and convenience.

By blending a premium coffee experience with a familiar neighborhood feel, the brand speaks directly to consumers who want a memorable coffee stop without sacrificing speed or authenticity.
