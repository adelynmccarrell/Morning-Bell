DIGITAL MARKETING GROUP PROJECT

Our Campaign Strategy

Connecting Morning Bell Coffee Roasters with more coffee lovers in Ames and surrounding communities.

01 / GOAL SELECTION

Building Local Brand Awareness

The primary goal of our digital advertising campaign is to increase brand awareness for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters in Ames, Iowa, and the surrounding communities. Through this campaign, we want to reach potential customers who may not be familiar with Morning Bell and encourage them to visit the coffee shop. By increasing brand awareness, we hope to attract new customers, build stronger connections with the local community, and ultimately increase sales.

02 / TARGETING

Reaching Our Community

Our primary target audience consists of individuals between the ages of 18 and 35, with a secondary audience between the ages of 35 and 60. The campaign will target all genders, primarily focusing on individuals living in Ames, Iowa, and the surrounding communities. Our audience includes Iowa State University students, faculty, university staff, college graduates, working professionals, and local residents. Students within our target audience generally have lower to middle incomes, while working professionals typically have middle to upper-middle incomes.

We want to reach individuals who regularly drink coffee and value quality, convenience, and supporting local businesses. Our advertisements will focus on websites and digital content related to coffee, local businesses, Iowa State University, student life, and the Ames community.

03 / THE HOOK

Great Coffee. Right Here in Ames.

Our campaign will focus on highlighting Morning Bell Coffee Roasters as a local coffee shop that offers quality coffee while supporting the Ames community. Our main message will emphasize the convenience of having a local coffee shop where students, professionals, and residents can enjoy their favorite coffee drinks. To capture attention, we will use engaging visuals of coffee drinks and messaging that encourages customers to choose a local business. Our goal is to make Morning Bell stand out as a welcoming coffee shop that fits into the everyday routines of our target audience.

04 / FINANCIAL ESTIMATES

Starting With a $300 Budget

For our digital advertising campaign, we would recommend starting with a budget of approximately $300 to increase awareness of Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. According to the 2026 WordStream benchmarks cited in our campaign planning, display advertisements in the restaurant and food industry have an average click-through rate (CTR) of 0.12% and an average cost per click (CPC) of $4.18.

$300Proposed Budget
0.12%Assumed CTR
$4.18Assumed CPC
$5.00Illustrative CPM

At a $4.18 CPC, the $300 budget would produce approximately 72 clicks. Separately, at an assumed $5.00 CPM, the same budget would buy approximately 60,000 impressions. Since our primary goal is brand awareness, we would focus on reaching as many potential customers as possible rather than expecting immediate purchases. We would also monitor conversions, such as online purchases or other measurable customer actions, although actual campaign data would be needed to establish a reliable conversion rate (CVR).

Planning note: These are two alternative budget estimates, not one combined forecast. At 60,000 impressions and a 0.12% CTR, the campaign would yield approximately 72 clicks, implying a CPC of about $4.17. Benchmark figures should be checked against the original source before final submission.
